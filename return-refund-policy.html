Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

We want you to contact us promptly if an order arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered.

Report a problem

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at WhatsApp +91 94122 44955. Include your order number and clear photos or video of the package and product.

Eligibility and review

Each request is reviewed based on the issue, product condition and available order details. Please do not send an item back before receiving return instructions from our team. Products damaged through use, mishandling or normal wear may not qualify.

Refunds and replacements

If a return, replacement or refund is approved, our team will confirm the next steps and applicable timeline with you. Refunds, where approved, will generally be sent to the original payment method; for cash-on-delivery orders, we will contact you to arrange an appropriate refund method.

Order cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation. Cancellation may not be possible after an order has been dispatched.
