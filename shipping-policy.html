Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Orders are processed after the order details and payment method (where applicable) are confirmed.

Dispatch and delivery estimates

Delivery time can vary by product availability, destination, courier service, weather and other factors. Any estimated delivery date shown at checkout or communicated by our team is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Address and contact details

Please provide a complete delivery address and an active mobile number. If details are incorrect or the courier cannot reach you, delivery may be delayed or the shipment may be returned.

Shipping charges and tracking

Any shipping charges and tracking details available for an order will be communicated during checkout or through order updates.

Delayed, lost or damaged shipments

If your parcel is delayed or arrives damaged, contact us at WhatsApp +91 94122 44955 with your order number and supporting photos where relevant. We will review the issue with the delivery partner as appropriate.
